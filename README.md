# Richard Freedman — Digital Supplements

This repository hosts musical examples, data visualizations, and interactive HTML supplements that accompany various publications and projects by Richard Freedman (Haverford College) and collaborators. Many of the visualizations are produced with [CRIM Intervals tools](https://github.com/HCDigitalScholarship/intervals).

Each project lives in its own folder, linked below.

## Publications with a Detailed Guide

These folders contain multiple figures and interactive files, and have their own README with a full guide and commentary.

### Echoes of Josquin: Palestrina's Missa Benedicta es and the Digital Ear

Richard Freedman, published in *Die Tonkunst* 19/4 (2025).

[View guide and figures](palestrina_josquin/readMe.md)

### Digital Tonalities: Exploring the Sounds of Renaissance Music through the Imitation Mass

Richard Freedman, published in *Die Tonkunst* 20 (2026).

[View guide and figures](tonkunst_tonalities/README.md)

### Lasso Takes a Second Look: An Imitation of an Imitation Mass?

Marcel Klinke (University of Heidelberg) and Richard Freedman, published in *Early Music*.

[View guide and figures](daser_lasso/README.md)
